# Baseline evaluation interpretation

## UK-to-US inference-only transfer

The LaTeX thesis's US transfer table uses the recorded `results.overall` fields
in the following evaluation artifacts. Values are averaged across the matched
training seeds; the skill spread uses the sample standard deviation. No score
is reconstructed by loading a checkpoint or rescoring the prediction arrays.
The generated `baselines/results/ALL_RESULTS.md` is absent from this checkout,
so the recorded JSONs are the numerical sources for this update.

These `all` evaluations apply fixed UK-trained models to every eligible US
plant. Their source manifests and per-plant entries define the scored population.
The quality exclusions are recorded in [the protocol](../knowledge/protocol.md).
This setting is distinct from the earlier fixed US split and from leave-one-plant-out
fitting within the US. It must not be described as either of those protocols.

Reduced-payload multi-anchor fusion has higher overall skill than spatial-token
fusion in each paired seed and lower mean overall NMAE. Spatial-token fusion
retains lower mean ramp NMAE. The multi-anchor advantage varies across sites,
and the seed spread is substantial. The recorded vision-off controls lower
mean overall and ramp errors for both token-based models; ramp errors fall in
every seed. Their between-model ranking consequently does not establish a
benefit from transferred imagery. The larger US payload variant shows positive
vision-off degradation in its sole run, with lower skill than the reduced arm.

A plausible explanation for the overall ranking reversal is that distributing
the token budget across coarser daily views reduces sensitivity to source-specific
recent visual detail, at the cost of detail useful within the UK. This remains
a hypothesis: the comparisons jointly change payload, age, and temporal depth,
while the dataset shift jointly changes region and sensor. Neither source
invariance nor beneficial use of older US images has been established.

The US panels are generated by [make_traces.py](../manuscript/scripts/make_traces.py)
from the corresponding seed-42 NPZ exports. They select the largest fully valid
observed end-to-end swing at each of the first three plant IDs in lexicographic
order, without using model errors. The plots illustrate selected horizons,
and do not supply replacement aggregate scores.
